Lark

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Lark is a music player for music files you own. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Lark server that receives your data, and Lark does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

The music files you add, their details (titles, artists, covers, lyrics), your playlists, likes, play history and settings are stored in your browser's private storage on your own device. They are never sent to the developer.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Lark asks Google for permission to use the drive.file scope. With it, Lark can only create, read, change and delete the files it creates itself, in a folder called "Lark" in your own Google Drive. Lark cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Lark uses this access only to keep your library the same on your devices: it uploads your music files, covers and library details to that folder and downloads them on your other devices. It also reads your Google account's email address, to show you which account is connected. This data travels directly between your browser and Google; it is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Lark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • To stop syncing, open Lark → Settings → Sync with Google Drive → Disconnect. You can also remove Lark's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the synced copy, delete the "Lark" folder in your Google Drive.
  • To delete everything on a device, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.
